# InterSystems

> InterSystems provides data platforms and healthcare information
> technology for mission-critical applications. Its principal offerings
> support data management, interoperability, analytics, artificial
> intelligence, healthcare, financial services, and supply chain operations.

InterSystems IRIS is the company's general-purpose data platform.
InterSystems IRIS for Health adds healthcare-specific capabilities and
support for healthcare information standards. For version-specific
technical information, use the official InterSystems documentation.

## Data Platforms and AI

- [InterSystems IRIS](https://www.intersystems.com/products/intersystems-iris/): Cloud-first data platform for high-performance, AI-enabled applications.
- [Generative AI with InterSystems IRIS](https://www.intersystems.com/products/intersystems-iris/generative-ai/): Semantic search, generative AI, and related capabilities.
- [InterSystems IRIS Cloud Services](https://www.intersystems.com/products/intersystems-iris-cloud-services/): Fully managed cloud services based on InterSystems technology.
- [InterSystems Data Studio](https://www.intersystems.com/products/intersystems-data-studio/): Managed, low-code access to distributed enterprise data.

## Healthcare Platforms and Interoperability

- [InterSystems IRIS for Health](https://www.intersystems.com/products/intersystems-iris-for-health/): Healthcare data platform supporting major healthcare standards.
- [InterSystems FHIR Services](https://www.intersystems.com/intersystems-fhir-services/): Managed infrastructure for accessing and using FHIR data.
- [InterSystems Health Connect](https://www.intersystems.com/products/health-connect/): High-performance healthcare integration engine.
- [HealthShare Unified Care Record](https://www.intersystems.com/products/healthshare/unified-care-record/): Aggregated, normalized, and deduplicated health record.
- [InterSystems EMPI](https://www.intersystems.com/products/intersystems-empi/): Enterprise identity-resolution and person-matching solution.
- [InterSystems OMOP](https://www.intersystems.com/resources/intersystems-omop/): FHIR-to-OMOP data pipeline and repository solution.
- [InterSystems Payer Services](https://www.intersystems.com/products/healthshare/payer-services/): Healthcare interoperability services for U.S. payer requirements.
